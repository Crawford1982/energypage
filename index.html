Scotland pays its wind farms to stop generating. Spillway shows where, when and how often.

The grid can't carry all of Scotland's wind power south. When a bottleneck is full, the grid operator pays farms behind it to switch off, and pays gas plants elsewhere to make up the gap.

Why it matters

  • £1.5bn paid to switch off wind farms and fire up gas instead, across Britain in 2026 by early October, already more than all of 2025. Octopus Energy, Wasted Wind tracker
  • 39% of the wind power northern Scotland could have generated in 2025 was switched off, 8.8 TWh in all. Montel, reported by Energy Voice
  • Late 2020s before the transmission upgrades that relieve the bottlenecks arrive. Until then, demand that can sit behind them and use spare power has real value. Modo Energy

How these numbers are checked

    Comparing sites for flexible demand?

    A Spillway location assessment compares up to three candidate sites using the historical record, so you can decide which deserve further development spend.

    • Which grid zone each site sits in, and how confident that mapping is
    • How often curtailment happens behind its bottleneck, for how long, and in which months and hours
    • Whether the site falls inside a NESO demand flexibility catchment
    • Planned network changes that could shrink the opportunity
    • What the evidence doesn't establish, and the questions an engineering study needs to answer
